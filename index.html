About

I'm a third-year Applied Mathematics student specializing in Business Mathematics at Universitas Pelita Harapan, with a strong interest in quantitative finance, statistical modeling, and data-driven decision making. I enjoy applying mathematical methods, programming, and analytical thinking to solve complex problems and transform data into meaningful insights.

Currently, I am conducting my undergraduate thesis on the impact of geopolitical conflicts on energy-sector stock performance in Indonesia, Malaysia, and Thailand using an longitudinal data analysis methodology. I am also preparing a conference paper on oil and gold price forecasting, exploring how statistical and machine learning approaches can improve predictive performance in financial markets.

Beyond research, I have gained experience as a Teaching Assistant for Computer Applications , and have actively taken on leadership roles in student organizations and university events. These experiences have strengthened my technical expertise, communication, teamwork, and project management skills. As I continue to grow, I aspire to build a career in finance or other data-driven fields, where I can apply mathematical modeling, programming, and analytical thinking to solve complex problems and create meaningful impact.
