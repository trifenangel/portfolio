Forecasting Oil and Gold Prices with Regime and Structural Demand Features

Overview

This research evaluates forecasting models for one-step-ahead prediction of oil and gold prices, incorporating regime indicators and structural demand features to improve predictive performance. Linear Regression, XGBoost, and Random Forest are compared, with XGBoost achieving the lowest errors (price-level MAPE of 1.82% for oil and 0.74% for gold).

Key Takeaways

  • Built and compared Linear Regression, XGBoost, and Random Forest forecasting models.
  • Engineered regime indicators and structural demand features for commodity price prediction.
  • Evaluated models out-of-sample, achieving price-level MAPE of 1.82% (oil) and 0.74% (gold).
  • Analyzed forecast errors under varying market-stress conditions.
