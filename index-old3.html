papers & publications

01

Forecasting Oil and Gold Prices with Regime and Structural Demand Features

Conference Paper · ICTIIA 2026, Surabaya · Co-author

This study investigates one-step-ahead forecasting of WTI crude oil and gold prices over 2018–2025, with out-of-sample evaluation from 2022 to mid-2025. Linear Regression, XGBoost, and Random Forest are compared using cross-market signals, regime indicators, and global EV adoption as a structural demand proxy. XGBoost achieves the lowest errors, with price-level MAPE of 1.82% for oil and 0.74% for gold.

Accepted for Presentation
